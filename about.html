Who We Are

Aegis Facility Care is a premier integrated facilities management provider, dedicated to optimizing the operational performance of commercial real estate. We manage the built environment so that our clients can concentrate on their core business activities without interruption.

With decades of collective experience, our specialized teams bring technical expertise, unwavering standards, and modern technology to every facility we manage.

  • Comprehensive facility assessments and tailored strategies.
  • 24/7 dedicated support and emergency response capabilities.
  • Commitment to sustainability and eco-friendly practices.
